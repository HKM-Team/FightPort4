The ports are built on SacredFire / bbport (GNSB), shadPS4, and OptiScaler (by nitec). Each port's own licenses folder
(games\UFC<n>\licenses) lists the libraries it contains and their licenses.

OptiScaler (v0.1.33-final, by nitec) is licensed under the MIT license and injected into
games\UFC4\bin\winmm.dll to provide Universal Upscaling (AMD FSR 1.0 - 4.1.1, Intel XeSS 1.3 - 2.0,
NVIDIA DLSS 3.5 - 4.5), Frame Generation (FSR 3.1 FG, XeFG, Multi-Frame Generation 2X - 6X)
and DLSS 5 Neural Rendering.
